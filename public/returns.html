Returns & Exchanges

Important Policy Update

Refund / Exchange / Return is ONLY applicable for damaged products.

To request a return or exchange for a damaged product, please follow these steps:

  • Click on the WhatsApp Chat icon at the bottom right corner of the screen.
  • Send us a clear continuous video of opening the parcel along with photos of the damaged product.
  • Our support team will verify and approve the request.

Note: Requests without an unboxing video will not be entertained.
